# Collaboration expectations

Treat contributors and learners with respect. Discuss ideas and evidence, give specific constructive feedback, and accept correction.

Harassment, discriminatory abuse, threats, personal attacks and sharing another person's private information are unacceptable. Keep issues and reviews relevant to the project.

The repository owner may remove inappropriate content or restrict participation. Report concerns through an established private owner contact; if none exists, request a private route without publishing sensitive details. No specific response time is promised.
